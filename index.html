About

A founder’s long view.

My entrepreneurial journey began at 17, and since then I’ve dedicated my career to creating sustainable businesses, leading high-performing teams, and delivering impactful projects across the public and private sectors.

Through Habrick Construction I specialize in government tenders, public infrastructure, and construction. The Royal Developers brings residential, commercial, and government development from planning and budgeting through execution. Civic Services LLC supports clients in the US, Australia, and the UAE with marketing, software, and outsourcing.

I hold a BBA from the University of Central Punjab — a foundation in strategy, finance, operations, and leadership that I pair with hands-on industry experience.
